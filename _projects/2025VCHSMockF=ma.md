In retrospect, the difficulty of this exam is quite a bit higher than that of a typical F=ma. Accordingly, the projected "USAPhO cutoff" is 12/25, although it's frankly just not very realistic. I wouldn't recommend taking this test as a timed full-length mock unless you're exhausted all of the past F=ma exams and all of the more realistic student-made mocks[^1] out there. However, I do think that this exam illustrates some relatively novel ideas and functions well as a practice problem set for F=ma preparation. I was the primary author of every problem except for #4 and #14.

The problems can be found [here](https://awang0826.github.io/problemwriting/2025_VCHS_Mock_Fma.pdf){:target="_blank"}, and the solutions are [here](https://awang0826.github.io/problemwriting/2025_VCHS_Mock_Fma_Exam_Solutions.pdf){:target="_blank"}.
