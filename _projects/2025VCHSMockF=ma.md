In retrospect, the difficulty of this exam is quite a bit higher than that of a typical F=ma. Accordingly, the projected "USAPhO cutoff" is 12/25, although it's frankly just not very realistic. I wouldn't recommend taking this test as a timed full-length mock unless you're exhausted all of the past F=ma exams and all of the more realistic mocks[^1] out there. However, I do think that the problems illustrate some nice new ideas, and that the exam functions well as a practice problem set for F=ma review.

The problems can be found [here](http://www.unexpected-vortices.com/sw/rippledoc/quick-markdown-example.html){:target="_blank"}, and the solutions are [here](https://awang0826.github.io/awang0826.github.io/_projects/2025_VCHS_Mock_F=ma_Solutions.pdf){:target="_blank"}.

[^1]: I find the 2024 UHS and 2024 PhODS exams more realistic than this test.
