2025 VCHS Mock F=ma

January 19, 2026

In retrospect, the difficulty of this exam is quite a bit higher than that of a typical F=ma. Accordingly, the projected “USAPhO cutoff” is 12/25, although it’s frankly just not very realistic. I wouldn’t recommend taking this test as a timed full-length mock unless you’re exhausted all of the past F=ma exams and all of the more realistic student-made tests1 out there. However, I do think that this exam illustrates some relatively novel ideas and functions well as a practice problem set for F=ma preparation.

The problems can be found here, and the solutions are here. I was the primary author of every problem except for #4 and #14.

The live contest took place over Zoom on 9/21/2025, and you can see the score distribution below.
